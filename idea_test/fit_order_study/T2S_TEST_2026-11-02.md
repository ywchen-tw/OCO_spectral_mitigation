# t2s test: two-τ order-2 fit with slope (2026-11-02)

## Model

Per band, ordinary least squares, all coefficients free, no bounds, no floor:

    ln T = c + a1·x − k1·τ_dry − k1w·τ_H2O + ½·k2·τ²        τ = τ_dry + τ_H2O,  x ∈ [−1, 1]

plus a2·x² for SCO2 over land (land_fraction ≥ 80), as in the agreed SCO2 model.
τ_H2O = frac_h2o·τ (frac NaN → 0; O2A uses `o2a_frac_h2o`, present in the Doppler τ files and in
`_doppler_fit_chunk`'s frac_slab). Formal 1σ from `_lin_se` (residual-scaled; understates
scene-correlated error).

* SCO2: identical to the agreed model `_fit_am_sco2` (bitwise, see below).
* WCO2: the `_fit_am_wco2` k2diag design with k2 kept (k2_t2s ≡ k2diag_am bitwise).
* O2A: replaces the nonlinear floor fit `fit_am_o2a` (c + a1·x + ln[(1 − f)e^(−k1τ) + f]) and splits τ.

Code: `src/spectral/cumulant_fit.py` — `fit_t2s(td, tw, x, y, land)`, `t2s_fit_chunk(j, tau_slab,
lnT_slab, frac_slab, land_chunk)` (same signature and inputs as `_doppler_fit_chunk`),
`T2S_FIELDS`, `T2S_MODEL`. Not part of `FIT_SCHEMA`; nothing calls it in production.

## Production unchanged

`t2s_bit_identity.py` loads a baseline copy of `cumulant_fit.py` next to the edited one.
On 120 synthetic soundings × 3 bands × 1014 channels (float64 and float32, masked channels, NaN frac,
starved soundings), `_doppler_fit_chunk`, `_extra_fit_chunk` (with and without frac), `_fit_chunk`,
`fit_am_o2a`, `_fit_am_wco2`, `_fit_am_sco2` give bitwise-equal outputs (NaN = NaN). `FIT_SCHEMA`,
`FIT_FIELDS`, `EXTRA_FIT_FIELDS` are equal. Hash of `_doppler_fit_chunk` output: float64
`c5795485b009514d`, float32 `1e4e2e4bf5dc59ea`, the same against HEAD and the pre-t2s working copy.
Against the pre-t2s working copy all 24 function sources are also identical. Against HEAD three sources
differ only by the earlier `_fit_am_o2a` → `fit_am_o2a` rename.

## Slab (Appendix G x-z slab, O2A, 33 wavelengths)

The slab τ is O2 + H2O. The H2O share is not stored in slab_od.h5, but it can be recovered exactly:
`gas_od.py --h2o-split` reruns `layer_od` with the O2 density zeroed. O2-only + H2O-only reproduces
the stored od_layer to 1.7e-18. The result goes to `slab_od_h2o.h5`; frac_h2o ≤ 5.4 %, and only at
near-continuum wavelengths (τ_H2O ≤ 1.6e-3). k1w is therefore unconstrained in the slab (−93…+39,
σ 14–44). The no-split refit changes k1 by ≤ 0.014 and k2 by ≤ 0.0032 (all columns), so k1w does not drive the closure.
x and the masks are the same as the am slab estimator. The MC run-to-run std of k1 and k2 is ≤ 1e-3,
so the slab misfit is model error, not MC noise.

## Reading

* SCO2 and WCO2: t2s adds nothing new. SCO2 is the agreed model. WCO2 t2s is the am k2diag refit, and
  its k2 is negative in 98 % of soundings. Keeping k2 in WCO2 lowers k1 by 0.027 (median) and drops
  r(k1, am) to 0.70, for a 2 % rms gain.
* O2A: t2s is not a viable replacement for the floor fit.
  * It is never negative-k2 (0 % of 10365 soundings), but only because the positive k2 (0.11) takes
    the role of the floor. The ln T flattening at large τ is fitted as curvature, and k1 rises by 0.08
    to compensate (1.061 vs 0.977).
  * Its rms is 31 % higher than the am rms, and k1 correlates with am k1 at only r = 0.63.
  * On the slab, the floor-free k2 does not track the traced path variance. It sits at 0.10–0.14 in
    every clear column (MC var 0.003–0.010). Over the bright surface it falls in the shadow (0.138 →
    0.126) while the MC variance rises six-fold. r(k2, MC var) is −0.03 for all columns and −0.88 for
    clear columns (am k2diag 0.82 / 0.97, order7 0.61 / 0.99).
  * The bright-surface shadow does not lengthen t2s k1; it shortens it (1.131 → 0.828, −0.30, down to
    0.77 inside the band). The tracer says +0.027 in mean l′. am also gives a small wrong-sign drop
    (0.963 → 0.935, with f rising to 0.07). Only order7 lengthens (1.015 → 1.167).
  * t2s k1 tracks the dark-surface mean as well as am does (r 0.995 / 0.999).
